I start to go to TSI weekly seminars and department's colloquium if there is no time/schedule conflict and if I indeed interested in the topic.

This Tuesday I went to the seminar presented by Dr. Chris Carr from Princeton University. He talked about regulating the growth of galaxies through their circumgalactic medium (CGM). He introduced a simplified 
one dimensional analytic model of the CGM-galaxy connection which highlights the topic of Baryon Cycle. The he introduced the mass loading, energy loading and metal loading factors to point out that galactic winds with higher
energy and low mass loading heat and expand the CGM, minimizing cooling and regulating the gas supply of galaxies and their star formation. And he suggest the observating method by detecting the abundance ratio of N and O
in CGM. Then he remained some future topics to be explored. I am very interested in this topic. Visitor can contact me to get more detailed information that I took from this presentation. Or directly him.
